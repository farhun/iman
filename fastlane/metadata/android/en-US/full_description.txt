<p>Iman is a prayer companion for salah times, Quran reading, and dhikr.</p>
<p><b>What Iman offers</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Prayer times for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha, with the current and next prayer</li>
<li>Prayer and pre-prayer reminders, with a sound choice for each prayer: default, adhan, takbir, or silent</li>
<li>Home-screen widget with show/hide options and a customizable active-prayer color</li>
<li>Qibla compass</li>
<li>Quran reader with search, bookmarks, translations, tafsir, and audio</li>
<li>Tasbih collections with immersive counters and customizable bead styles</li>
</ul>
