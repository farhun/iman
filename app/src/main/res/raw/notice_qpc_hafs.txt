Uthmani Quran text and font

Text: QPC Hafs script (Ayah by Ayah). Font: KFGQPC Uthmanic Hafs.
Published by the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex (KFGQPC) and obtained from the Quranic Universal Library (QUL), qul.tarteel.ai.

KFGQPC makes its digital Quran text and fonts available for free use, including in software.
https://dm.qurancomplex.gov.sa/rights/
